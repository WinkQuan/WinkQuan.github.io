I am a PhD student in the [Faculty of Computing](https://encs.hit.edu.cn/) at [Harbin Institute of Technology](https://en.hit.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Xiaopeng Fan](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/xiaopengfan) and [Assoc. Prof. Wenrui Li](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/liwenrui).

I joined HIT in September 2024 and entered the PhD phase of the integrated master's–PhD program in Computer Technology in March 2026. Prior to this, I obtained a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from the College of Information and Management Science at [Henan Agricultural University](https://en.henau.edu.cn/).

My research interests lie in **embodied intelligence**, **vision-language-action (VLA) models**, and **reinforcement learning**.
